# Project Report

## Business Problem
A retail company needs better sales planning and demand visibility to reduce stock-outs and excess inventory.

## Objective
Analyze historical sales, identify trends and seasonal patterns, forecast future sales and present findings through an interactive dashboard.

## Data Collection
A realistic synthetic retail dataset was generated because the assignment does not provide a dataset. It contains more than 10,000 records.

## Cleaning
Missing values were handled, duplicate Order IDs removed, dates parsed, and sales recalculated from quantity, unit price and discount.

## Analysis
Monthly sales trends, product/category performance, seasonality, regional/channel performance and growth direction were analyzed.

## Forecast
A linear trend was fitted to monthly sales and extrapolated for six future months.

## Dashboard
The Streamlit dashboard contains Total Sales, Units Sold, Orders, Average Order Value, monthly trend, product/category charts, seasonality, forecast, filters, insights and data preview.
